Hauling Ass Info Checklist

This checklist gathers everything needed to build a clean website for Hauling Ass: hauling services, contact info, photos, and optional Shopify shirt sales.

Main goal: make the business easy to understand, easy to contact, and ready to sell shirts through Shopify if needed.

Recommended Starting Point

Start simple: a one-page hauling business website with a merch section that links to Shopify. The website shows the business. Shopify handles the checkout.
